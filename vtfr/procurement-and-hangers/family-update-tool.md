# Family Update Tool

In the Revit family editor, you can now add any of the **Victaulic Shared Parameters** to your custom families with a few clicks.

These parameters can make any Revit family more compatible with the toolbar's commands, annotation tags, and workflows.

> **Tip:** Click the **Show More** link at the bottom left of the window to see all available parameters. Many are designed to work with the [Procurement Tool](procurement-tool.md) to enhance the listing of items on a Bill of Material.
